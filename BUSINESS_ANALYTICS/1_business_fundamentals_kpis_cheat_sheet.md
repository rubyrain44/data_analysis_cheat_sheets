# BUSINESS FUNDAMENTALS & KPIs CHEAT SHEET

# 1. BUSINESS & COMMERCIAL ACUMEN

## How Businesses Make Money

REVENUE:
Money generated from selling products or services.

Revenue = Price × Quantity Sold

COSTS:
Expenses incurred to operate a business.

Fixed Costs:
Costs that generally don't change with production volume.
Examples: Rent, salaries, insurance.

Variable Costs:
Costs that change with production volume.
Examples: Materials, packaging, shipping.

PROFIT:
Money remaining after expenses.

Profit = Revenue - Total Costs

GROSS PROFIT:
Revenue - Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

OPERATING PROFIT:
Gross Profit - Operating Expenses

NET PROFIT:
Revenue - All Expenses - Taxes - Interest

PROFIT MARGIN:
Profit / Revenue × 100

IMPORTANT:
- Revenue is not the same as profit.
- High revenue doesn't necessarily mean high profitability.
- Businesses monitor costs to protect margins.
- Understand gross, operating and net profit.

## Common Business Models

- B2B: Business sells to another business.
- B2C: Business sells directly to consumers.
- Subscription: Customers pay recurring fees.
- Marketplace: Connects buyers and sellers.
- E-commerce: Products or services sold online.
- Freemium: Basic service free, premium features paid.

## Business Departments

SALES:
Revenue generation, pipeline and conversion.

MARKETING:
Customer acquisition, campaigns and brand awareness.

FINANCE:
Revenue, costs, budgeting and profitability.

OPERATIONS:
Efficiency, productivity and delivery.

HUMAN RESOURCES:
Hiring, retention and employee performance.

CUSTOMER SUCCESS:
Customer satisfaction, retention and churn.

IMPORTANT:
Data Analysts often work across multiple departments.

# 2. BUSINESS KPIs & METRICS

A KPI (Key Performance Indicator) measures progress toward a business objective.

## Financial KPIs

Revenue:
Price × Quantity Sold

Gross Profit:
Revenue - COGS

Net Profit:
Revenue - Total Expenses

Gross Profit Margin:
Gross Profit / Revenue × 100

Net Profit Margin:
Net Profit / Revenue × 100

Operating Margin:
Operating Profit / Revenue × 100

Revenue Growth:
(Current Revenue - Previous Revenue)
 / Previous Revenue × 100

Cost of Goods Sold (COGS):
Direct costs associated with producing or purchasing goods sold.

Return on Investment (ROI):
(Net Return / Investment Cost) × 100

## Sales KPIs

Average Order Value (AOV):
Total Revenue / Number of Orders

Average Selling Price (ASP):
Revenue / Units Sold

Sales Growth:
(Current Sales - Previous Sales)
 / Previous Sales × 100

Sales Conversion Rate:
Number of Sales / Number of Leads × 100

Units Per Transaction:
Units Sold / Number of Transactions

## Customer KPIs

Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):
Acquisition Costs / Number of New Customers

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV):
Estimated total value generated by a customer over their relationship with the business.

Simple CLV estimate:
Average Revenue Per Customer × Customer Lifespan

Customer Retention Rate:
(Ending Customers - New Customers)
 / Starting Customers × 100

Customer Churn Rate:
Lost Customers / Starting Customers × 100

Repeat Purchase Rate:
Repeat Customers / Total Customers × 100

## Marketing KPIs

Click-Through Rate (CTR):
Clicks / Impressions × 100

Conversion Rate:
Conversions / Visitors × 100

Cost Per Click (CPC):
Advertising Spend / Clicks

Cost Per Lead (CPL):
Marketing Spend / Leads

Cost Per Acquisition (CPA):
Marketing Spend / Acquisitions

Return on Ad Spend (ROAS):
Revenue Attributed to Advertising / Ad Spend

## Operational KPIs

On-Time Delivery Rate:
Orders Delivered On Time / Total Orders × 100

Return Rate:
Returned Orders / Total Orders × 100

Inventory Turnover:
COGS / Average Inventory

Employee Turnover Rate:
Employees Leaving / Average Employees × 100

Capacity Utilization:
Actual Output / Maximum Capacity × 100

IMPORTANT:
- Define every KPI before calculating it.
- Use consistent reporting periods.
- Understand the numerator and denominator.
- Check whether metrics are counts, averages, rates or totals.
- Different departments may define similar metrics differently.
